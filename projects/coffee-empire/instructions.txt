# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: COFFEE EMPIRE
## Complete Incremental Tycoon Browser Game

You are an experienced game developer, economy designer, UI/UX designer and JavaScript engineer.

Your task is to design and build a complete, polished, fully playable incremental idle game called **COFFEE EMPIRE**.

This is not a prototype, MVP, demonstration or phased development exercise. Build the complete game with all specified systems, content, progression mechanics and functionality.

# 1. GAME CONCEPT

Coffee Empire is a large-scale incremental business simulation game.

The player starts with a tiny coffee cart, limited capital and a basic coffee machine.

Over time, the player builds an enormous international coffee corporation by expanding into:

- Coffee shops
- Coffee chains
- Coffee plantations
- Agricultural operations
- Bean processing
- Coffee roasting
- Manufacturing
- Packaging
- Warehousing
- Transportation
- Wholesale distribution
- Supermarket retail
- Coffee subscriptions
- Coffee equipment manufacturing
- International markets
- Corporate acquisitions

The ultimate objective is to become the world's largest coffee corporation.

The game should combine the addictive progression of Cookie Clicker and Adventure Capitalist with the economic depth of a business management simulator.

The player should always have something to purchase, improve, unlock, optimize or work towards.

## Core principles

- Numbers continuously increase.
- Businesses generate passive income.
- Upgrades provide satisfying multipliers.
- New industries unlock throughout progression.
- Production chains create interconnected economic systems.
- Automation becomes increasingly powerful.
- Prestige provides long-term replayability.
- Offline progression rewards returning players.
- The game must remain enjoyable over extended play sessions.

The game must be playable entirely offline after loading.
